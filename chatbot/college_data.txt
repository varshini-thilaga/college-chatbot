Eligibility  
• For admission to B.E./B.Tech  Courses, candidates should have completed 12 
years of schooling (equivalent to 10+2 system of Indian Education). They must 
have studied and obtained a pass with Good Grades as per their systems of 
education with Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics as their subjects in the 
qualifying examination.  
• Candidate should have studied in English Medium or English as one of the 
subjects in the qualifying Examination.  
• Candidates must have passed the qualifying examination, i.e. (10 + 2) of Indian 
System or its equivalent (completed 12 years of school education).  
• The Candidate should have passed in all the subjects and scored a minimum 
average of 45 percentage in Mathematics, Physics and Chemistry.  
• If the candidate has grade certificates, he/she has to produce either the actual 
marks or equivalent marks for the respective grades. In case equivalent mark 
range is given for grades by the respective examination authorities, only the 
minimum marks applic able to the grades in the eligible subjects (Mathematics, 
Physics and Chemistry) will be taken into account.  
• The candidate should provide equivalent mark or mark range for grades from the 
Principal.  
• The candidate has to provide a pass certificate, if it is not clear from the mark 
sheet whether the candidate has passed all the subjects. Mark sheets with 
voluminous data or confusing formats will not be accepted.  
Selection Procedure  
The selection is purely provisional and is subject to the confirmation from the Anna 
University, Directorate of Technical Education, Chennai and All India Council for 
Technical Education (AICTE), New Delhi. Since the medium of instruction is ENGLISH in 
Sri Eri Eshwar College of Engineering, only those candidates who have studied in the 
English Language medium / English as one of the subjects in the qualifying degree alone 
will generally be considered for admissions.  
 
 
• The candidates have to indicate their order of Preference of Branches of study in 
the application.  
• Mode of selection - Selection Committee will scrutinize the applications 
received based on eligibility criteria and intimate the applicants.
